The procedural human-skinned.glb asset is generated by this project's
scripts/generate-avatar.ts, scripts/remesh-human.py and
scripts/create-correctives.ts, followed by scripts/refine-avatar-blender.py,
from project-owned anatomical shape definitions.
This dedication also covers artifacts/human-continuous.obj,
artifacts/human-refined.blend, artifacts/human-authoring.glb and surface-report.json.
It contains no downloaded third-party textures, motion clips, or model meshes.

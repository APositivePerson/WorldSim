The JSON recordings are derived from real publicly available measurements, not synthetic human observations.

- Tim J. van der Zee, Paolo Tecchio, Daniel Hahn, Brent J. Raiteri: Gastrocnemius medialis muscle ultrasonography dataset. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26502868. CC BY 4.0. Participant 1 dynamometry and EMG subset. Filtered, resampled, normalized against dedicated MVCs, converted to dorsiflexion-positive SI coordinates, and windowed as documented in each trial.
- Claudiane A. Fukuchi, Reginaldo K. Fukuchi, Marcos Duarte: A public data set of overground and treadmill walking kinematics and kinetics of healthy individuals. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.5722711. CC BY 4.0. Participant 01, left side, T01/T08 phase ensemble subset. Converted degrees to radians, plantarflexion-positive moments to dorsiflexion-positive moments, and mass-normalized moments/forces to SI. Gait phase is retained; no physical time is fabricated.
